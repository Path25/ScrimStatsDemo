## Founder-approved Elite proposition

### Buyer and recurring job

- **Primary buyer:** the coach, manager, or programme lead accountable for whether a competitive team improves between weekly practice blocks.
- **Weekly job:** set one intentional pre-scrim objective; attach or record the evidence available from practice; turn it into an accountable follow-up; review whether the next session addressed it; and make missing evidence explicit.
- **Minimum credible outcome:** staff can run that loop without losing objectives, evidence, action ownership, or next-session follow-through across separate schedules, VOD notes, and spreadsheets. It does not assert that ScrimStats caused a performance improvement.

### Tier and product boundary

- **Free:** run the team consistently through core operations.
- **Pro:** understand practice and prepare better through the established captured/recorded-game, analytics, scouting, Draft, and coaching workflow.
- **Elite:** run a staff-owned improvement programme. The first differentiated system is the practice-development loop in WO-033; its controls are staff-facing, while other authorised roles may receive only the documented read-only projection.
- The visible development loop remains Elite-only. Shared internal foundations may reduce duplication but do not create Free/Pro screens, mutations, or plan access. Any future cross-tier exposure requires a separate founder-approved work order.

### Evidence, claim, and disqualifying-gap boundary

- Current WO-033 evidence proves a named non-customer Elite QA fixture and direct hosted role/tenant checks only. It is **not** evidence that the capability is generally customer-available, supportable, adopted, or retention-driving.
- Never claim automated improvement, performance gains, complete programme management, top-tier intelligence, provider-backed data, Discord/calendar automation, organisation hierarchy, or wellness support from this proposition.
- Elite self-serve purchase, Billing copy, checkout, entitlement, support readiness, and public availability remain separately governed by WO-031. They cannot be promoted from this internal proposition alone.

### Sequence and downstream decision rule

1. **WO-033:** establish and independently verify the customer-safe Elite practice-development loop before treating it as an Elite outcome.
2. **WO-034:** only then add a weekly command centre as a truthful summary of existing objectives, evidence, actions, and upcoming practice—not a second source of truth or dashboard rewrite.
3. **WO-035:** complete source/rights/freshness boundaries for patch, official-data, top-tier-example, and learning-resource candidates. Research may proceed, but no provider-backed customer feature follows without its source decision.
4. **WO-036:** build repeatable opponent-preparation playbooks only after both the practice loop has customer-scope evidence and the relevant source boundary is approved.
