## Problem and user impact

Elite needs a durable reason to pay beyond isolated feature locks. The proposed differentiator is a staff-facing improvement programme: objectives, evidence, accountable actions, and follow-through across weekly practice. Without a defined buyer/job and evidence standard, an Elite roadmap risks becoming a collection of unrelated dashboards and integrations.

## Scope

- Define the target Elite buyer, recurring weekly job, minimum credible outcome, and evidence required before customer claims.
- Decide the product sequence: practice-development loop first; weekly command centre second; source-backed preparation third.
- Define what remains explicitly outside the first Elite proposition: organisation parent hierarchy, wellness data, generic AI claims, and unverified automation.
